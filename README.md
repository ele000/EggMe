# Project Description

The application is a recipe-sharing platform designed to connect people who are passionate about cooking. Users can discover new recipes, search and filter them according to their needs, save their favorite dishes, and share their own creations with the community.

The goal of the project is to facilitate the **discovery, creation, and sharing of community-based culinary recipes**. The platform does not only support recipe publishing, but also allows users to share their experiences through reviews, photos, and practical tips.

## Main Features

### User Authentication and Profiles

- user registration and login;
- personal profile management;
- management of culinary preferences, including:
	- preferred types of cuisine;
	- dietary restrictions;
	- favorite ingredients;
	- cooking role, such as food lover, home cook, content creator, or professional chef.

### Recipe Management

Registered users can create and manage their own recipes through create, update, and delete operations. Each recipe can include:

- an estimated cost range;
- preparation difficulty;
- preparation and cooking time;
- number of servings;
- a list of ingredients with the required quantities;
- detailed step-by-step instructions;
- nutritional information, such as calories;
- a dish category and a related cuisine or culture;
- dietary restriction labels, such as vegan, gluten-free, pescatarian, or halal.

Users can also import or duplicate a recipe already published on the platform, starting from an existing preparation to create their own variation. The author of the original recipe is notified when it is duplicated.

### Recipe Search, Exploration, and Favorites

The platform allows users to explore community recipes and search for them using filters such as:

Ingredients, number of servings, cost range, calories, dietary restrictions such as vegan, gluten-free, pescatarian, or halal, dish type such as appetizer, main course, or dessert, cuisine type such as Italian, Asian, or barbecue.

Users can save recipes to their personal favorites and organize them into custom collections, for example to create a tasting menu.

### Reviews, Photos, and Tips

Authenticated users can interact with the community after trying a recipe. They can:

- publish a review;
- attach photos of the result;
- share tips, variations, and practical suggestions, including recommended practices and things to avoid;
- record recipes they have prepared, keeping track of their past cooking experiences.

### Notifications

Users receive notifications about:

- recipe duplications or new recipes published starting from one of their own recipes;
- recommended recipes, whether newly published or already available, selected according to their profile preferences;
- new reviews received on their published recipes.
